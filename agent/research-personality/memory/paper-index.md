## Notes

- **Lain 2020**: the full text is the NIH author manuscript from PMC. Neither the PMC manuscript nor the Springer article page lists any supplementary material (no ESM section, no supplementary-material in the PMC XML), so there is no supplement to download.
- **PoppiE (NCT04586348)**: no results paper has been published yet (the SAP says data collection was due to end July 2026). The set includes the protocol (Best 2023, BMJ Open) and the statistical analysis plan (Sullivan 2025, Trials).
- **Williams 2017**: PMID 28557747 is the Williams iodine RCT (Pediatrics; I2S2 trial, UK/Scotland, not NZ). PMID 30802939 is Walsh et al. 2019, a Cochrane review, and was not used.
- **Feyrer 2017**: the primary file is the PMC author manuscript of the published JEEA article. The Nov 2015 working paper (FPW_2015_11_07.pdf) is also kept and converted. An older NBER w19233 (2013) copy is in work/ but was not converted.
- **Levie 2019**: Europe PMC and PMC returned 404, so the PDF came from the free OUP copy.
- Unpaywall rejected the `joshua@example.com` address (HTTP 422, "use your own email"), so OA locations were looked up with OpenAlex, which uses the Unpaywall data.

### Notes on the follow-up items

- **Item 1 mismatch**: DOI 10.1089/thy.2022.0103 is **Zhang K et al. 2022, Thyroid** 32(8):962–971, "Trends in Iodine Status Among U.S. Children and Adults…" (Shanghai Jiao Tong authors). The requested title, "Iodine inadequacy is prevalent and has continuously increased from 2001 to 2018 in the U.S.", belongs to a different paper: **Lieberman HR, Mathews RS, Fulgoni VL 2025, J Nutr** 155(11):3886–3895, doi 10.1016/j.tjnut.2025.09.017. Both are listed. Both are closed access.
- **Walsh 2019**: the DOI given, `.pub4`, does not exist (Crossref 404). The PubMed record 30802939 and the PDF are `.pub3`, and `.pub3` is the DOI used here.
- **Georgieff 2018**: the DOI given, 10.1111/apa.14255, belongs to a different paper (Kato et al., sleep and electronic devices). The correct DOI for PMID 29468731 is 10.1111/apa.14287.
- **Hernández 2017**: the first author is Valdés Hernández MC, and the cohort is older adults (Lothian Birth Cohort 1936).
- **Ghassabian 2018**: the full title is "Disruption in Thyroid Signaling Pathway: A Mechanism for the Effect of Endocrine-Disrupting Chemicals on Child Neurodevelopment" (Front Endocrinol).
