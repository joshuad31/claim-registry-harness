# Setting up NotebookLM

1. Create one notebook and upload every converted source.
2. Open **Configure Chat** (the three-dot menu). Choose **Custom**, paste the Archivist prompt your assistant gives you, choose **Longer**, and save.
3. Make two or more copies of the notebook. Each copy answers in parallel; three to six keep a quest under three hours. Copies add speed, not evidence.
4. Open Configure Chat in every copy and check it. Copies sometimes lose the setting.
5. Give your assistant the link to each copy.

Never:

- use "Discover sources", or import its results;
- save answers to notes;
- tell NotebookLM your hypotheses or what you hope to find.

A separate Google account for this work is a good idea. You sign in yourself; your assistant never needs your password.
