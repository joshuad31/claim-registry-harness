# Who does what

- **You** own the question and the rival answers. Nobody accepts them for you.
- **Your assistant** is the AI you are talking to. It guides you and runs the commands.
- **The Archivist** is NotebookLM. It holds the sources and answers one question at a time. It never sees your hypotheses.
- **The Investigator** is an AI that writes the questions. It never sees the scores.
- **The Manager** is an AI that scores the answers and suggests what to ask next. It never touches NotebookLM.
- **The coach** helps you choose how to word your claim, and shows what each wording is likely to cost you.

The separation is the point. Because the Archivist does not know what you hope to find, the Investigator has to ask questions good enough to pull the right passages out.
