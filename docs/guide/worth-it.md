# Is it worth the work?

Claim Registry asks for hours to days of your attention. That is worth paying in some situations and not in others.

**It tends to be worth it when:**

- the answer will change a decision: something to raise with a doctor, an argument to make, a piece you will publish;
- the sources disagree, or the experts do, and you cannot tell who is right;
- you have asked an AI and received confident answers that contradict each other;
- you will have to show your reasoning to someone who doubts you;
- more than one explanation fits what you have seen, and they lead to different actions.

**It is probably not worth it when:**

- you want a quick fact, or an overview of a subject;
- nobody seriously disputes the answer;
- you need something today.

In those cases, ordinary AI research is the better tool, and your assistant can do that instead.

**You do not have to commit to all of it at once.** Start with the two answers that are hardest to tell apart and a small pile of sources. The first result shows whether a bigger run is worth it. Everything you do is saved in the project folder, so you can stop and come back.
