# What you get for it

- **A result you can check.** Every finding links to its quotation and to the question that found it. You do not have to trust the AI.
- **The other side, found on purpose.** The questions look for what would hurt your answer as hard as for what would help it.
- **A map of what is missing.** Each part of your claim is marked supported, contested, supported only if you accept a stretch (for example from animals to people), or not addressed by any source.
- **The crux.** The few findings that one answer needs and its rival cannot explain.
- **A claim sized to the evidence.** If the strong version of your claim does not hold, you can see which narrower version does.
- **Something you can hand over.** A doctor, a lawyer or a sceptic can follow each finding back to its source.

Ordinary AI research gives you an answer. This gives you the evidence for and against each answer, in a form other people can inspect.
