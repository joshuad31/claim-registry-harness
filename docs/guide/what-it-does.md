# What it does, and what it does not

You bring a question that has more than one plausible answer, and a pile of sources. Claim Registry tests the rival answers against what the sources say, and shows you the exact passages behind the result.

It can:

- find the passages in a large pile of sources that bear on your question (the needles in the haystack);
- check every quotation word for word against its source;
- show which parts of each answer the sources support, contradict or never address;
- help you restate your claim so that it matches what the evidence can carry;
- end with "the sources cannot decide" when that is the honest result.

It cannot:

- prove that something is true. It reports what this set of sources supports;
- get past a paywall. Your assistant does help you find papers and downloads the ones that are free to read ([Getting the papers](getting-papers.md));
- make up for sources you did not include;
- replace an expert's judgment of whether a study was done well.
