# Getting the papers

This is the longest step. Your assistant does most of the legwork.

**What your assistant does.** It searches for studies that bear on each rival answer, including the ones that count against the answer you prefer, and proposes a short list. You decide what goes in. It then downloads every paper that has a free, lawful copy and files it in your project.

**Why it sometimes cannot download a paper.** It should tell you which of these applies:

- **The publisher sells it and no free copy exists.** Try a public or university library, or email the author; your assistant can draft the email.
- **It is free to read, but the site blocks automatic downloads.** Your assistant gives you the link. You open it and save the PDF into the project folder.
- **It needs your sign-in,** for example through a library. You sign in and download it. Your assistant never handles your password.
- **Your assistant has no internet access where it runs.** It gives you links instead.
- **Only a summary (abstract) is online.** A summary is not the paper, and it is marked as such.

**A paper you cannot get is recorded as a gap.** The result then shows what is missing, which is better than pretending. Do not use pirate sites.
