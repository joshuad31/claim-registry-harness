3. Authors' Conclusions: Prediction versus Causation
[CLINICAL] [INFERRED] Vogel et al. (2023):
Associative Parameter: The authors conclude that systemic inflammatory markers (CRP, IL-6) during early withdrawal represent surrogate predictive biomarkers associated with muscle dysfunction, rather than proven causal drivers (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Abstract; Discussion).
Causation Limitations: They explicitly state: "This is an observational study and therefore demonstrates only associations and not causation" (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Discussion). They note that whether low-grade inflammation directly mediates myopathy or represents a parallel immunometabolic consequence of central obesity/hyperglycemia remains unproven (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Discussion).
