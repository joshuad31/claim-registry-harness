5. Synthesis and Key Implications
Evidence Type Differentiation:
[CLINICAL]: Continuous IV pentoxifylline (500 mg/4 h) in healthy human volunteers completely abolishes endotoxin-induced plasma TNF-α surges (24 vs. 38 pg/mL, P<0.02), but fails to attenuate fever (38.9 °C), myalgia, headache, nausea, tachycardia, or IL-6 surges (~1,200 vs. ~1,500 pg/mL, P=NS) (Zabel 1989).
