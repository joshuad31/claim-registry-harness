Model & Species: Young Yorkshire swine (45–60 kg) subjected to continuous intravenous live Pseudomonas aeruginosa sepsis (ridings1994.pdf.md, ABSTRACT & MATERIALS AND METHODS).
Dosing Routes & Regimens: Intravenous (IV) pentoxifylline (PTX) delivered as a 20 mg/kg bolus followed by a 6 mg/kg/h continuous IV infusion (ridings1994.pdf.md, MATERIALS AND METHODS).

[PRECLINICAL] Waxman et al., 1991 (waxman1991.pdf.md):
Model & Species: Male Sprague-Dawley rats (300–350 g) subjected to fixed-volume arterial hemorrhage (phlebotomy of 21 mL/kg over 5 min) followed by a 15-minute unresuscitated shock delay (waxman1991.pdf.md, MATERIALS AND METHODS).
Dosing Routes & Regimens: Intravenous (IV) pentoxifylline administered as a 25 mg/kg bolus plus a 25 mg/kg 45-minute infusion (total 50 mg/kg IV) dissolved in lactated Ringer's resuscitation fluid (infused at twice the shed blood volume over 45 min) vs. saline placebo (waxman1991.pdf.md, MATERIALS AND METHODS).
