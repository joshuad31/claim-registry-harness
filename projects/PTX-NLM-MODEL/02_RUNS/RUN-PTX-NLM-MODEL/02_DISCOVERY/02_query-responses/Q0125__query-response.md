Key Pathophysiological and Clinical Directives
[MECHANISM] Both studies demonstrate the potent tonic regulatory control that anti-inflammatory therapies and endogenous steroids exert over systemic and tissue immune cascades (pucheu-haston2014.pdf.md, Discussion; Papanicolaou1996_Withdrawal_Syndrome_and.md, Abstract; Discussion).
[INFERRED] Critical clinical boundaries emerge:
Target Specificity of Pentoxifylline: Pentoxifylline selectively inhibits late-phase cellular recruitment (eosinophils, total inflammatory cells) and dermal induration without suppressing immediate IgE-mediated mast cell histamine release (pucheu-haston2014.pdf.md, Abstract; Discussion).
Washout Vigilance: The therapeutic anti-inflammatory action of pentoxifylline washes out within 7 days of discontinuation, requiring continuous dosing for sustained clinical benefit (pucheu-haston2014.pdf.md, Results; Discussion).
Steroid Withdrawal Surge: Abrupt cortisol withdrawal unmasks an acute plasma Interleukin-6 surge that directly drives steroid withdrawal symptoms (fever, fatigue, nausea), which are rapidly reversed upon reinstating physiological replacement (Papanicolaou1996_Withdrawal_Syndrome_and.md, Abstract; Discussion).
