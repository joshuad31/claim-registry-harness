[PRECLINICAL] Rodent antiviral protection: 100 to 200 mg/kg in Japanese encephalitis virus models (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Pleiotropic effects of pentoxifylline - Antiviral effects).
Outcomes, Values, and Statistics:
[CLINICAL] ACS Trial: Oral PTX 400 mg/day for 6 months significantly reduced interleukin-6 (IL-6), IL-12, and tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α) while increasing anti-inflammatory IL-10 (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Pleiotropic effects of pentoxifylline - Anti-inflammatory effects).

Consistency of Effective Dose Range:
[CLINICAL] In humans, the effective oral anti-inflammatory dose is highly consistent across clinical indications (ACS, CKD, diabetic nephropathy, membranous nephropathy, COVID-19), centering on 400 mg 2 to 3 times daily (800–1200 mg/day) (lin2004.pdf.md, Review Article; mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Pleiotropic effects...). In a 70-kg adult, this corresponds to 11.4 to 17.1 mg/kg/day.
[PRECLINICAL] In canine models, anti-inflammatory efficacy requires 20 mg/kg TID (60 mg/kg/day) (pucheu-haston2014.pdf.md, Treatment with pentoxifylline), which represents a 3.5- to 5.2-fold higher nominal dose than human regimens.
[INFERRED] Consequently, raw mg/kg dose consistency across species is poor due to rapid canine drug elimination and first-pass metabolic variations.

4. Synthesis and Key Implications
Evidence Type Differentiation:
[CLINICAL]: Oral PTX (800–1200 mg/day) consistently reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, IL-12, TNF-α) in human ACS (Mostafa-Hedeab 2022), reduces proteinuria (2.7 to 1.1 g/day, P<0.05) and TNF-α in diabetic nephropathy (Lin 2004), and improves lymphocyte counts (+64.25%) in COVID-19 (Mostafa-Hedeab 2022), with low adverse event rates (~3% GI/dizziness).
[PRECLINICAL]: In dogs (Pucheu-Haston 2014, N=6), oral PTX (60 mg/kg/day for 29 days) significantly impairs late-phase IgE-mediated cutaneous induration at 6 h and 24 h (P<0.05), reduces total dermal inflammatory cells (-55.4%, P<0.01) and eosinophils (-84.2%, P<0.01), and inhibits mast cell degranulation; these anti-inflammatory effects fully reverse 7 days post-discontinuation.
[MECHANISM]: PTX acts as a non-selective PDE inhibitor, elevating intracellular cAMP/PKA, downregulating NF-κB/AP-1 transcription, reducing pro-inflammatory cytokines/chemokines (TNF-α, IL-6, MCP-1), and blocking Akt-mediated cell proliferation and TGF-β1/CTGF fibrogenesis.
[INFERRED]: Extrapolating canine preclinical anti-inflammatory models to human clinical care requires allometric BSA dose conversion and active metabolite (M1, M5) profiling, as nominal canine dosing (60 mg/kg/day) is substantially higher than human clinical dosing (800–1200 mg/day).
[NO SOURCE]: Head-to-head unbound target-tissue metabolite concentrations (M1, M4, M5) are missing across all three sources.
Key Implications for Practice:
Oral PTX (400 mg 2–3x/day) provides consistent, well-tolerated systemic anti-inflammatory and microvascular benefit across chronic renal, cardiovascular, and inflammatory conditions.
Preclinical allergic and cutaneous models demonstrate that PTX specifically targets late-phase leukocytic infiltration rather than immediate histamine release, supporting its role as a chronic maintenance therapy rather than an acute rescue agent.
