1. Intravenous Pentoxifylline in Surgical Severe Sepsis (staubach1998.pdf.md)
[CLINICAL] Trial Design & Sample Size: Staubach et al. (1998) conducted a prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial evaluating 51 surgical intensive care unit (ICU) patients presenting with severe sepsis (staubach1998.pdf.md, Abstract; PATIENT SELECTION). Patients were randomized within 12 hours of diagnosis to receive either continuous intravenous (IV) pentoxifylline (27 patients) or matching 0.9% saline placebo (24 patients) (staubach1998.pdf.md, Abstract; PATIENT SELECTION). Septic shock was present at enrollment in 85.2% (23/27) of pentoxifylline-treated patients and 87.5% (21/24) of placebo controls (P=0.81) (staubach1998.pdf.md, Table 2. Severity of Sepsis at Enrollment).
[CLINICAL] Dosing Regimen & Duration: Pentoxifylline was administered as a continuous IV infusion at 1 mg/kg body weight per hour (maximum 1,800 mg/day) for up to 28 days, or until ICU discharge or death (staubach1998.pdf.md, Interventions; PATIENT SELECTION).
[CLINICAL] Measured Serum Exposure: Serial gas-liquid chromatography quantified steady-state serum concentrations of parent pentoxifylline (PTX) and its primary active metabolite, 1-(5'-hydroxyhexyl)-3,7-dimethylxanthine (BL 194) (staubach1998.pdf.md, CLINICAL EVALUATION; RESULTS):

[INFERRED] Ambulatory Outpatient Context Supports Chronic Oral Trials:
Route and Regimen Feasibility: staubach1998.pdf.md evaluated continuous IV infusions in an acute critical care setting (ICU), which is inapplicable to outpatient management (staubach1998.pdf.md, Interventions). In contrast, Pentoxifylline-in-patients-with-ulcerative-colitis...pdf.md provides direct clinical trial evidence supporting a chronic oral regimen using standard 400 mg controlled-release tablets administered twice daily (800 mg/day) (Pentoxifylline-in-patients-with-ulcerative-colitis...pdf.md, Study design; Discussion).
Demonstrated Long-Term Tolerability: The ulcerative colitis trial establishes that 6 months (24 weeks) of continuous oral pentoxifylline is well tolerated in outpatients, exhibiting an adverse event profile statistically indistinguishable from placebo/mesalamine monotherapy (P>0.40 across all reported side effects) and maintaining patient retention over extended administration (Pentoxifylline-in-patients-with-ulcerative-colitis...pdf.md, Table 6; Discussion).
Targeted Mucosal and Systemic Efficacy: Chronic oral administration achieved concurrent systemic (TNF-α, IL-6, ESR) and local mucosal (fecal MPO, colonic STAT-3 downregulation, ZO-1 tight-junction upregulation) anti-inflammatory efficacy, validating the oral route for chronic mucosal and vascular remodeling trials (Pentoxifylline-in-patients-with-ulcerative-colitis...pdf.md, Table 3; Table 5).
