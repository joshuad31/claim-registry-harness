A. mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md (Narrative Review of Pentoxifylline in Inflammatory Disorders & COVID-19)
[CLINICAL] Population Studied: Literature review covering diverse human clinical conditions, including COVID-19 pulmonary hyperinflammation, sarcoidosis, alcoholic hepatitis, diabetic neuropathy, erectile dysfunction, Peyronie's disease, and osteoradionecrosis (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
[MECHANISM] Doses, Pharmacokinetics, and Exposure:
Parent pentoxifylline (PTX) exhibits an oral bioavailability of 20% to 30% due to extensive hepatic first-pass metabolism (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
Elimination half-life is short, approximately 1.6 hours (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
Plasma protein binding is high, with 45% of circulating drug bound to the erythrocyte membrane (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
Clearance occurs via hepatic metabolism followed by renal excretion of metabolites (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
[CLINICAL] Adverse Events: Common reported clinical side effects include palpitations, flushing, headache, and cardiac arrhythmias (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
[PRECLINICAL] Toxicity Threshold: Systemic toxicity is reported at an acute dose threshold of 80 mg/kg, precipitating severe hypotension, facial flushing, and central nervous system convulsions (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
[CLINICAL] Drug Interactions & Contraindications:
Stated to possess no serious drug-drug interactions in general clinical use (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).
In clinical trials of diabetic nephropathy, PTX successfully combined with angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs) to modulate angiotensin II (Ang II) signaling and attenuate renal cytokine expression without compromising blood pressure control (mostafa-hedeab2022.pdf.md, Introduction).

[CLINICAL] Doses & Combination Regimens: Evaluated MTOPS (finasteride + doxazosin), CombAT (dutasteride + tamsulosin 0.4 mg), tamsulosin + tadalafil 5 mg, tadalafil 5 mg + finasteride 5 mg, and NEPTUNE II (solifenacin + tamsulosin) (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > Combination Therapy).
[CLINICAL] Adverse Events by Class:
Alpha-Blockers: Dizziness, orthostatic hypotension, retrograde/abnormal ejaculation, rhinitis, Intraoperative Floppy Iris Syndrome (IFIS) during cataract surgery, and emerging epidemiological signals connecting tamsulosin to increased dementia risk (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > Alpha Blockers).
5-ARIs: Erectile dysfunction/impotence, decreased libido, reduced ejaculate volume, gynecomastia, and a prospective risk of depression/self-harm within the initial 18 months of treatment (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > 5-alpha Reductase Inhibitors).
PDE5Is: Headaches, flushing, dyspepsia; in men >75 years, higher numerical rates of diarrhea and dizziness (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > Phosphodiesterase Inhibitors).
Anticholinergics: Dry mouth, constipation, blurry vision, acute urinary retention, and central M1-mediated cognitive decline / cumulative dose-dependent dementia (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > Anticholinergics).
Combination Therapy: 46.8% adverse event rate in NEPTUNE II (dry mouth, constipation, dyspepsia) and 1.1% acute urinary retention (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > Combination Therapy).
[CLINICAL] Drug Interactions, Contraindications, & FORTA Ratings:
PDE5Is + Nitrates: Strictly contraindicated due to potentiation of cyclic GMP-mediated systemic vasodilation, resulting in life-threatening hypotension (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > Phosphodiesterase Inhibitors).
Alpha-Blockers + Antihypertensives: Co-administration increases the risk of orthostatic blood pressure drops and syncope. Under the Fit fOR The Aged (FORTA) scale, non-selective alpha-blockers (alfuzosin, doxazosin, terazosin) are classified as Class D ("Avoid") due to severe orthostatic hypotension and cardiac arrhythmia risks, whereas selective agents (tamsulosin, silodosin) are classified as Class C ("Caution") (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Review of Medications > Alpha Blockers).
FORTA "Beneficial" (Class B) Agents: Only finasteride, dutasteride, and fesoterodine achieved a Class B rating for older men (bortnick2019.pdf.md, Summary; Conclusion).
