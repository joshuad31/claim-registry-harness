4. Defensible Effect Size and Control Design Parameters for a New Clinical Trial
[INFERRED] Synthesizing natural history trajectories (chung2016.pdf.md), combination therapy trial outcomes (Comparison-between...pdf.md), and biomarker moderation patterns (raison2013.pdf.md) establishes the necessary design parameters for a new clinical trial evaluating anti-inflammatory or microvascular add-on agents (e.g., pentoxifylline):
Control Design Architecture:
Active Background Control: Uncontrolled or open-label designs are non-defensible because 25% to 50% of untreated men experience spontaneous 5-year LUTS/OAB remission and 58% experience nocturnal polyuria resolution without treatment (chung2016.pdf.md, EPIDEMIOLOGY... > Overactive bladder...). The control design must be a 1:1 randomized, double-blind, active-controlled parallel trial (Active Monotherapy + Add-On vs. Active Monotherapy + Placebo) (Comparison-between...pdf.md, 2.3 | Study design).
Biomarker Enrichment: As demonstrated in raison2013.pdf.md, unselected trials mask efficacy and risk harm (d=−0.82, NNH=6.25 in low-CRP patients). Prospective stratification by baseline systemic inflammatory markers (hs-CRP>3 or 5 mg/L) is required (raison2013.pdf.md, RESULTS; COMMENT).
Defensible Effect Size & Sample Size:
Target Incremental Effect Size: A net IPSS total score reduction of 3.0 to 3.6 points above active background control (standard deviation ≈4.0 points), or a standardized effect size of d=0.40 to 0.50 (Comparison-between...pdf.md, TABLE 4; raison2013.pdf.md, RESULTS).
Required Sample Size: To detect a standardized effect size of d=0.45 with 80% statistical power at two-tailed α=0.05, a new trial requires N=78 patients per arm (N=156 total evaluable patients) within the biomarker-enriched target stratum.
