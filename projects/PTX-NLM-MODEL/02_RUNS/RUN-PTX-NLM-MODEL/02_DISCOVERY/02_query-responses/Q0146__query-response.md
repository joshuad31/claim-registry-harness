Including unselected LUTS patients dilutes treatment signals because therapies targeting detrusor perfusion or contractility will fail in pure BOO or severe acontractility (raison2013.pdf.md, RESULTS; rademakers2016.pdf.md, PREOPERATIVE CHARACTERIZATION...).
Pre-Specified Treatment-by-Subgroup Interaction Tests: The statistical analysis plan must pre-specify formal interaction tests (e.g., treatment × time × detrusor contractility or hs-CRP) to validate whether treatment efficacy is moderated by baseline characteristics, guarding against post-hoc data dredging (raison2013.pdf.md, RESULTS).
Dual Co-Primary Objective & Subjective Endpoints: Trials must pre-specify co-primary endpoints combining validated patient-reported outcomes (IPSS total, IPSS-V voiding subscore, IPSS-S storage subscore, QoL) with objective urodynamic/ultrasound parameters (Q
max
	​

, PVR, voiding efficiency). Symptom scores alone cannot differentiate detrusor failure from outlet obstruction (rademakers2016.pdf.md, CONCLUSION; Comparison-between-combination-of-tamsulosin-and-Pentoxifylline-versus-tamsulosin-alone-in.pdf.md, 2.1 | Outcome measures).
Subgroup-Calibrated Statistical Powering: Sample size calculations must be powered to detect treatment differences within target sub-phenotypes (e.g., DU patients or biomarker-high cohorts), rather than relying on overall sample power that becomes underpowered when stratified (raison2013.pdf.md, DISCUSSION; rademakers2016.pdf.md, PREOPERATIVE CHARACTERIZATION...).
Off-Treatment Durability / Offset Phase: Protocols must pre-specify a post-treatment withdrawal phase (e.g. 4–12 weeks post-discontinuation) to distinguish temporary microvascular/rheological smooth-muscle relaxation from permanent detrusor tissue remodeling (Comparison-between-combination-of-tamsulosin-and-Pentoxifylline-versus-tamsulosin-alone-in.pdf.md, 4 | DISCUSSION).
