# SYNTHETIC — Field trial of foliar silicate in drought-stressed wheat (field2023)

> Fabricated for the worked example. Not a real study. Do not cite.

## Results

Foliar silicate increased grain yield of drought-stressed wheat in field trials by eighteen percent. The increase was measured at a foliar rate of two millilitres per litre. The yield increase was larger when drought was severe.

## Methods

The trial compared a foliar silicate spray with a water control in field plots at two sites. Plots were twelve square metres and the trial was replicated four times at each site.

## Limitations

The trial covered two sites and one season, and the silicate product concentration was not independently verified.
