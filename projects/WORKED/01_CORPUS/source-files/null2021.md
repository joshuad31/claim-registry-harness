# SYNTHETIC — Multi-site field trial reporting a null yield result (null2021)

> Fabricated for the worked example. Not a real study. Do not cite.

## Results

Foliar silicate did not increase grain yield of drought-stressed wheat in field trials. These field trials at a foliar rate of two millilitres per litre reported no yield difference between silicate and control plots. Grain protein content was similar between treatments.

## Methods

Four field sites were sown with the same cultivar and sprayed twice with foliar silicate or a water control. Yield was measured at maturity from twelve square metres per plot.

## Limitations

The trial cannot exclude a yield effect smaller than its detection limit.
