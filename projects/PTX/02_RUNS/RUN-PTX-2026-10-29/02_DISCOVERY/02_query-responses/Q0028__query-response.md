## Exact answer

```text
In kruuse2000.pdf.md, a double-blind, randomized, crossover trial evaluated single-dose intravenous (IV) pentoxifylline (PTX; 300 mg infused over 60 minutes) versus isotonic saline placebo in 10 healthy subjects (7 women, 3 men; n=9 for rCBF analysis due to one input artifact) (kruuse2000.pdf.md, Material and methods, Results).
Hemodynamic and Vascular Outcomes
[CLINICAL] Regional Cerebral Flow (rCBF): PTX 300 mg IV failed to change rCBF in the middle cerebral artery territory or global CBF at 55 min or 115 min (pCO2-corrected peak baseline change: +0.2% PTX vs. +9.6% placebo; P>0.05) (kruuse2000.pdf.md, Results, Main study).
[CLINICAL] Large-Artery Measures: Middle cerebral artery velocity (Vmca) decreased by 7.2% during PTX infusion (P=0.048), but did not significantly differ from placebo (P=0.14) (kruuse2000.pdf.md, Results, Main study). Superficial temporal artery diameter increased by 9.0% (P=0.002), but contrast with placebo was not statistically significant; radial artery diameter remained unchanged (kruuse2000.pdf.md, Results, Main study).
[CLINICAL] Systemic Hemodynamics & Second Messengers: PTX significantly increased heart rate (131.0% of baseline at 60 min vs. 106.8% placebo; P<0.001), systolic blood pressure (P=0.02), and jugular vein plasma cAMP (+53.0%; P<0.001), while plasma cGMP was unchanged (kruuse2000.pdf.md, Results, Table 2, Figure 4).
Ischemic Bladder Application
What It Tests: [INFERRED] Demonstrates that acute IV PTX raises circulating cAMP and induces mild systemic sympathomimetic effects without producing direct macro-arterial vasodilation or hyper-perfusion in uncompromised, non-ischemic baseline vessels (kruuse2000.pdf.md, Discussion).
What It Does NOT Test: [NO SOURCE] It does not evaluate pelvic microvascular resistance, detrusor ischemic hypoxia, red blood cell rheology in microvessels, or acute voiding recovery in an obstructed or ischemic bladder subgroup (kruuse2000.pdf.md, Material and methods).
```
