## Exact answer

```text
[MECHANISM] Neural Lesions & Signaling Defects: Reviewed treatments target neurogenic underactive bladder (UAB) caused by pontine micturition center disruption, sacral parasympathetic nucleus lesions, impaired urothelial/C-fiber afferent sensing, or peripheral efferent axonal degeneration (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 3.1. Neurogenic). Underlying etiologies include cerebrovascular accidents, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson’s disease, diabetic autonomic neuropathy, and spinal cord injuries (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 6. Risk factors).
[CLINICAL] Pharmacological Successes vs. Failures:
Succeeded: Urethral botulinum toxin A (BTX-A; 76–97% success reducing outlet resistance) (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 7.3.11. Botulinum toxin), α1-adrenoreceptor antagonists (tamsulosin, urapidil + cholinergic; increased Qmax and reduced PVR) (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 7.3.2. Alpha-adrenoreceptor antagonists, 7.3.12. Combination therapy), 5-HT4 agonists (prucalopride), and acotiamide (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 7.3.7. Muscarinic receptor antagonists, 7.3.8. 5-Hydroxytryptamine (5-HT)).
Failed: Direct muscarinic agonists (bethanechol, carbachol) and acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (distigmine in RCTs) failed to improve detrusor contractility or emptying over placebo due to tissue unresponsiveness, receptor downregulation, or dose-limiting toxicity (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 7.3.1. Parasympathomimetics).
[CLINICAL] Populations & Exposure Constraints: Evaluated in neurogenic cohorts (CVA, MS, SCI, diabetic cystopathy) and post-surgical urinary retention (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 6. Risk factors). Interventions were constrained to short durations (4–6 weeks), low non-toxic dosing, and absolute dependence on intact efferent nerve terminals (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 7.3.1. Parasympathomimetics).
[INFERRED] Flawed Transfer Assumption: Assuming non-neurogenic BPH or shy-bladder men share neurogenic efferent defects or intrinsic detrusor unresponsiveness (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 1. Introduction). BPH emptying failure is driven by mechanical prostatic obstruction, while shy bladder stems from psychogenic external sphincter hypertonicity—neither is resolved by enhancing parasympathomimetic or neurogenic reflex pathways (ladi-seyedian2017.pdf.md, 1. Introduction, 9. Expert commentary).
```
