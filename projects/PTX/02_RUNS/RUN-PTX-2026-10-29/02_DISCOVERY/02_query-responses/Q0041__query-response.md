[CLINICAL] Postoperative Inflammatory Trajectory & Muscle Relation: In 80 patients (55 pituitary, 21 adrenal, 4 ectopic), curative surgery induced a low-grade inflammatory state lasting up to 1 year (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Methods > Patients, Figure 1). At 1 month post-op (n=66), median CRP increased 4.8-fold to 0.48 mg/dL (IQR 0.14–0.90) from 0.10 mg/dL preoperatively (P≤.001) (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Abstract). In a 25-patient matched subset, IL-6 increased 4.2-fold to 7.2 pg/mL (P≤.001) (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Results > Remission phase...). Inflammation was exacerbated by obesity (BMI >30 kg/m²: 1-month IL-6 9.2 vs 4.0 pg/mL in BMI <30; P=.03) and HbA1c (rs=0.302, P=.015) (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Table 2, Figure 3). Elevated 3-month CRP inversely predicted 12-month muscle function (normalized grip strength: rs=−0.371, P=.008) (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Figure 4).
[CLINICAL] Predictive Association vs. Causal Mediation: Observational correlations demonstrate that CRP and IL-6 serve as predictive biomarkers for steroid withdrawal myopathy, but do not establish direct causal mediation (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Discussion > Limitations, strengths, and outlook).
[INFERRED] Trial Measurement Schedule & Recovery Boundary: To separate early biomarker attenuation from delayed tissue recovery, a PTX trial requires serial inflammatory profiling during early treatment (1 and 3 months) alongside lagged functional evaluations at 6, 12, and 24 months. Systemic endocrine/myopathic withdrawal kinetics must remain distinct from local detrusor, prostatic, or bladder micturition mechanics (Vogel2023_Glucocorticoid_Withdrawal.md, Abstract).
