Inclusion criteria required CP symptoms for >3 months (frequency, urgency, voiding difficulty, upper abdominal or perineal discomfort), National Institutes of Health Chronic Prostatitis Symptom Index (NIH-CPSI) subscale scores ≥1 for pain and voiding, and persistent symptoms despite prior medical therapy with antibiotics and α-blockers for >6 months (he2010.pdf.md, PATIENTS AND METHODS). [CLINICAL] Urodynamic Phenotyping in He et al. 2010: Synchronous pressure-flow urodynamics with surface anal EMG electrodes, rectal balloon catheters, and fluoroscopic voiding cystourethrography demonstrated high-pressure, low-flow voiding driven by voluntary or involuntary external sphincter contraction during micturition (he2010.pdf.md, PATIENTS AND METHODS). Biofeedback Programs: Relaxation/Coordination vs. Strengthening
[CLINICAL] He et al. 2010 Biofeedback Protocol: Patients underwent an active neuromuscular re-education program focused strictly on pelvic floor muscle relaxation and detrusor-sphincter coordination, explicitly excluding pelvic floor muscle strengthening (PFMT/Kegels) (he2010.pdf.md, PATIENTS AND METHODS). Using anal surface EMG electrodes and 6F rectal catheters, patients were placed in an optimal voiding position (sitting, thighs spread, back straight, tilted slightly forward) and taught to perceive sphincter contraction versus relaxation (he2010.pdf.md, PATIENTS AND METHODS). They were trained to relax the perineal musculature while maintaining a flat abdominal pressure curve during voiding (he2010.pdf.md, PATIENTS AND METHODS). Sessions lasted ≈30 minutes, administered 2–3 times per week over a 10-week course, during which all antibiotics, α-blockers, and anticholinergics were suspended (he2010.pdf.md, PATIENTS AND METHODS).
[CLINICAL] Kaplan et al. 1997 Biofeedback Protocol: Biofeedback re-education utilized the Incare PRS 8900 unit, targeting sphincteric inhibition and pelvic floor relaxation rather than muscle strengthening (kaplan1997.pdf.md, MATERIALS AND METHODS). Alternative Explanations and Unmeasured Parameters
[MECHANISM] Strongest Alternative Explanations:
Acquired Behavioral / Psychogenic Habit State: Both author groups hypothesize that pseudodyssynergia represents an acquired, mislearned habit or physical somatic expression of emotional stress (he2010.pdf.md, DISCUSSION; kaplan1997.pdf.md, DISCUSSION). Kaplan et al. noted that 91% (39/43) of patients were firstborn men under high achievement anxiety, suggesting that an initial transient insult (such as acute prostatitis or urethritis) reinforced a dysfunctional pelvic floor bracing reflex that persisted long after parenchymal tissue inflammation resolved (kaplan1997.pdf.md, RESULTS, DISCUSSION).
Secondary Reaction to Chronic Pain / Inflammation: Nociceptive input from pelvic floor or prostatic inflammation may trigger protective reflex hypertonicity of the external urethral sphincter, creating a self-perpetuating cycle of elevated urethral pressure, intraprostatic ductal reflux, and recurrent voiding symptoms (he2010.pdf.md, DISCUSSION).
