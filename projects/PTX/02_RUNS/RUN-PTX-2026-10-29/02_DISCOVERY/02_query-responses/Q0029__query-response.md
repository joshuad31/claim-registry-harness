## Exact answer

```text
[CLINICAL] In Dezube 1993, oral pentoxifylline (PTX) 400 mg controlled-release TID (every 8 hours) was administered for up to 3 weeks (1–3 weeks) to 7 adult cancer patients (ECOG performance status ≤2; sample size n=7 treated on 8 occasions out of 15 screened) (dezube1993.pdf.md, Materials and methods; dezube1993.pdf.md, Results).
[CLINICAL] Measurement & Target Change: Target engagement was assessed by quantitating TNF-α mRNA levels (TNF/β-actin RNA ratio) in peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMC) via Northern/slot-blot laser densitometry, normalized against 8 healthy volunteers (dezube1993.pdf.md, Materials and methods). On all 3 treatment occasions where baseline TNF RNA was elevated (>1.0), oral PTX dramatically suppressed TNF mRNA accumulation within 1 week (dezube1993.pdf.md, Results). Circulating serum TNF protein release could not be detected or quantified reproducibly (dezube1993.pdf.md, Discussion).
[CLINICAL] Null Findings & Tolerability:
Null Target Effect: On all 5 occasions where initial TNF RNA levels were normal, PTX did not suppress TNF RNA below normal levels (dezube1993.pdf.md, Results).
Tolerability: Well tolerated with no severe adverse effects; mild nausea occurred in 2/7 patients (dezube1993.pdf.md, Abstract; dezube1993.pdf.md, Results).
[CLINICAL] Target Change vs. Clinical Functional Benefit: Molecular TNF RNA suppression disconnected from clinical functional benefit: 4/7 patients reported improved appetite and daily activity, and 2 patients gained >5% body weight at 3 weeks—yet both weight-gain responders belonged to the cohort with normal pretreatment TNF RNA levels (dezube1993.pdf.md, Abstract; dezube1993.pdf.md, Results).
[NO SOURCE] Limits of Transfer to Bladder Dysfunction: Dezube 1993 evaluated PBMC cytokine gene expression in systemic cancer cachexia; it did not measure prostate tissue, bladder wall microvasculature, detrusor contractility, or lower urinary tract voiding endpoints (dezube1993.pdf.md, Abstract).
✨ Would you like to review how these systemic PBMC TNF RNA kinetics compare with local tissue inflammatory markers measured in animal renal or intestinal injury models?
```
