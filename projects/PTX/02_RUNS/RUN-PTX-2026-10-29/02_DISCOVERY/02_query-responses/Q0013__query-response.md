Primary Cohorts vs. Review Pooling
[CLINICAL] In s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, four randomized controlled trials (N = 318; 159 PTX, 159 placebo) evaluated pentoxifylline in major depressive disorder (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Abstract):
400 mg BID Regimens (800 mg/day):
El-Haggar 2018 (N = 80; +escitalopram 20 mg/d; 12 weeks): Discontinuation was 2/40 PTX vs. 2/40 placebo (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 6). Reported AEs included nausea (6 vs. 5), insomnia (6 vs. 8), and sexual dysfunction (3 vs. 5) (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 5).
TAM Mohammad 2024 (N = 100; +citalopram 20 mg/d; 12 weeks): Discontinuation was 3/50 PTX vs. 5/50 placebo (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 6). Reported AEs included headache (10 vs. 12), vomiting (10 vs. 12), and fatigue (8 vs. 4) (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 5).
Yasrebi 2021 (N = 70; PTX monotherapy in CAD; 6 weeks): Discontinuation was 3/35 PTX vs. 3/35 placebo (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 6). Reported AEs included dizziness (5 vs. 4) and sexual dysfunction (6 vs. 7) (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 5).
400 mg TID Regimen (1200 mg/day):
Farajollahi-Moghadam 2021 (N = 68; +sertraline 100 mg/d; 6 weeks): Discontinuation was 6/34 PTX vs. 6/34 placebo (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 6). Reported AEs included abdominal pain (5 vs. 3) and dizziness (5 vs. 4) (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 5).
Pooled Meta-Analysis: [CLINICAL] Pooled all-cause discontinuation showed no difference between groups: RR = 0.88 (95% CI [0.45, 1.74], 14/159 vs. 16/159) (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 6). Pooled AEs were statistically non-significant, including nausea (RR = 1.06 [0.55, 2.03]), dizziness (RR = 1.27 [0.61, 2.67]), and abdominal pain (RR = 1.67 [0.76, 3.66]) (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Fig. 5).
Ascertainment & Follow-up Limits
[CLINICAL] Follow-up was brief (6–12 weeks) with zero post-treatment follow-up to evaluate long-term safety (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Limitations). Ascertainment relied on self-reported psychiatric adverse events without urodynamic, prostatic, or continuous blood pressure monitoring (s00210-025-03845-1.pdf.md, Limitations).
