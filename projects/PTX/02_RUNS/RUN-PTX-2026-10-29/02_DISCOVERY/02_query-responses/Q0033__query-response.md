Overall Result & hs-CRP Response Pattern
[CLINICAL] In a 12-week trial of N=60 outpatients with treatment-resistant depression (30 infliximab [5 mg/kg IV], 30 placebo), infliximab showed no overall benefit over placebo in HAM-D score reduction (P=.92; overall response rate 50% vs 50%, P=.99) (raison2013.pdf.md, Abstract, Results).
[CLINICAL] A significant interaction occurred between treatment, time, and log baseline hs-CRP (P=.01) (raison2013.pdf.md, Abstract):
hs-CRP > 5 mg/L (n=22: 13 infliximab, 9 placebo): Favored infliximab (HAM-D score reduction 3.1 points greater than placebo; effect size 0.41; response rate 62% [8/13] vs 33% [3/9], P=.19) (raison2013.pdf.md, Results, Figure 3, Figure 6).
hs-CRP ≤ 5 mg/L (n=38: 17 infliximab, 21 placebo): Favored placebo (effect size -0.82; response rate 41% [7/17] vs 57% [12/21], P=.33) (raison2013.pdf.md, Results, Figure 6).
Challenge to Blanket Anti-TNF Benefit
[CLINICAL] Infliximab’s overall failure and its inferiority to placebo in low-inflammation patients (hs-CRP ≤ 5 mg/L) challenges blanket anti-TNF therapy, demonstrating that baseline inflammation is required for efficacy and that suppressing basal physiological cytokines may be detrimental (raison2013.pdf.md, Comment).
Testing Needed for PTX Voiding-Trial Selection
[INFERRED] Infliximab is a monoclonal anti-TNF antibody evaluated for psychiatric HAM-D scores, whereas pentoxifylline (PTX) is an oral phosphodiesterase inhibitor evaluated for lower urinary tract symptoms (IPSS, Qmax, PVR).
[INFERRED] Before an inflammatory marker can select a PTX voiding-trial subgroup, studies must establish:
A validated, lower urinary tract-specific baseline marker threshold (not extrapolated from psychiatric depression) [NO SOURCE].
Proven statistical interaction between baseline marker levels and PTX-induced voiding improvements [NO SOURCE].
