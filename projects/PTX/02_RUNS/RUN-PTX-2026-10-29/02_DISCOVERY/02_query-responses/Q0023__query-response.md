[CLINICAL] Male Selection & Denominators: 33 consecutive men (age >18, pelvic pain/discomfort ≥3 months, NIH-CPSI > 15) diagnosed with CP/CPPS type III were enrolled (cornel2005.pdf.md, Methods). 33 men were enrolled, and 31 completed treatment and were analyzed (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results).
[CLINICAL] Treatment Completion, Dropouts, and Nonresponse:
Dropouts (2/33, 6.1%): One patient dropped out because he failed to understand training instructions (baseline NIH-CPSI 21, elevated tone); the second patient stopped training after 3 sessions and failed to return (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results).
Completed Nonresponder (1/31, 3.2%): 30 of 31 completed patients (97%) showed total NIH-CPSI score improvement (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results). One patient worsened (+1 in pain, +1 in quality of life, micturition unchanged) (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results). The micturition subscore improved in 25/31 completed men (81%) (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results).
[CLINICAL] Biofeedback Exposure: Individual physical therapy sessions occurred weekly initially, then every 2–4 weeks for 6 to 8 total sessions (cornel2005.pdf.md, Methods). Exercises used an anal canal EMG probe (Pelvined 932) to record levator ani resting tone and guide contraction/relaxation (cornel2005.pdf.md, Methods).
[CLINICAL] Pelvic Floor Tone & Urinary Outcomes:
Pelvic Floor Tone: Mean resting EMG tone dropped from 4.8 μV (range 2.0–10.0) at baseline to 1.7 μV (range 0.5–2.8) post-treatment (p<0.001, documented in 18/31 men) (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results, Table 1).
Symptom Micturition Score: NIH-CPSI micturition subdomain score decreased from 5.1 (range 0–10) to 2.2 (range 0–6) (p<0.001) (cornel2005.pdf.md, Table 1).
[NO SOURCE] Measured Flow / Residual vs. Symptom-Only Effect: Uroflowmetry and post-void residual (PVR) urine were measured at baseline for diagnostic screening (cornel2005.pdf.md, Methods), but no post-treatment Qmax flow rates or PVR volumes were measured or reported (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results). The micturition benefit was evaluated strictly via subjective symptom scores (cornel2005.pdf.md, Results).
