[CLINICAL] Domains & Items: A 12-item scale spanning three dimensions: pelvic floor muscle training compliance (5 items), bladder training compliance (5 items), and urination diary recording (2 items) (s12912-023-01326-5.pdf.md, Construct validity).
[CLINICAL] Validation Population: 123 patients with urinary incontinence (88 males [71.5%], 35 females [28.5%]; mean age 58.57 ± 12.96 years; 99.2% with prior pelvic surgery) (s12912-023-01326-5.pdf.md, Characteristics of the study participants, Table 4).

[CLINICAL] Behavior vs. Beliefs: The 12 scale items measure self-reported training execution behaviors (PFMT, bladder training, diaries) (s12912-023-01326-5.pdf.md, Table 2). [NO SOURCE] Exposure & Nonresponse Distinction: The scale does not quantify physical pelvic-floor muscle contraction exposure (hold times or force) and cannot distinguish completed-treatment nonresponse from therapy abandonment (s12912-023-01326-5.pdf.md, Evaluation).
