[CLINICAL] Population, Design, Denominators, & Attrition: In a retrospective cohort study of 958 adult female urogynecology patients (nihms-1048633.pdf.md, Methods, Results), 35% (335/958) were referred to pelvic floor physical therapy (PFPT) (nihms-1048633.pdf.md, Results). Of those referred, overall attendance (≥1 visit) was 67% (224/335) and completion (≥3 visits or discharge) was 42% (141/335), yielding a 58% attrition/non-completion rate (nihms-1048633.pdf.md, Materials and methods, Results).
[CLINICAL] Multidisciplinary vs. Standard Care Effects:
Referral: Multidisciplinary clinic care increased referral rates compared to single-specialty care (55% [119/216] vs. 29% [216/742], p<0.001) (nihms-1048633.pdf.md, Results).
First Attendance: Same-day PFPT consultation during initial multidisciplinary evaluation significantly increased first visit attendance (91% [67/74] vs. 61% [157/259], p<0.001) (nihms-1048633.pdf.md, Results).
Completion Rate: Same-day multidisciplinary consult did not significantly change completion rates (49% [36/74] vs. 41% [105/259], p=0.15) (nihms-1048633.pdf.md, Results).
