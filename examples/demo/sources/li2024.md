# SYNTHETIC — Silicate improves root hydraulic conductance in a model plant under osmotic stress (li2024)

> Fabricated demonstration source. Not a real study. Do not cite as evidence.

## Abstract

We examined whether silicate treatment alters root hydraulic conductance in a
model plant species subjected to osmotic stress in hydroponic culture.

## Methods

Seedlings were grown in hydroponic solution with or without 1 millimolar sodium
silicate for 10 days. Osmotic stress was imposed with polyethylene glycol 6000 at
minus 0.4 megapascals. Root hydraulic conductance was measured with a pressure
chamber. Each treatment had 15 seedlings.

## Results

Silicate-treated seedlings maintained root hydraulic conductance at 0.62 times the
unstressed control value, compared with 0.38 in untreated stressed seedlings.
Aquaporin gene expression was higher in silicate-treated roots. Shoot dry weight
was 12 percent higher in treated stressed seedlings.

## Discussion

The result identifies a plausible water-transport mechanism for silicate under
osmotic stress. It does not establish a grain yield effect in any crop, and the
model species differs from wheat in root anatomy and silicon accumulation.

## Limitations

Hydroponic culture does not resemble field soil. The osmotic stress was constant
rather than a natural drying cycle, and no yield component was measured.
