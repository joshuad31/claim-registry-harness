# SYNTHETIC — Review of foliar silicate and wheat yield (review2019)

> Fabricated for the worked example. Not a real study. Do not cite.

## Findings

Field evidence on foliar silicate and wheat yield is inconsistent across trials. Some field trials report a yield increase, while others report no yield difference at the same foliar rate. The review did not identify a rate or timing explanation for the disagreement.

## Interpretation

The authors conclude that the field findings are inconsistent and that another field trial at a stated rate is needed.

## Limitations

This was a narrative review without a pooled effect size, and publication bias was not assessed.
