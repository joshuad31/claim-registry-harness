# Claim Registry

Read `AGENTS.md` in this folder and follow it. It says how to act for someone who wants to research
something, and sends you to `HANDOFF.md` if they want the harness itself changed.

The standing research personality is required at startup: `python cr.py personality` loads the full profile and every restored file in `agent/research-personality/`. `cr start` includes it. Read all lossless parts if output is truncated; do not treat this as an optional conversion skill.
